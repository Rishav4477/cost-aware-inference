RUNTIME DEPENDENCIES

Connected services

01

Policy gates first

Before scoring, the gateway filters providers by model alias, cloud allowlist, privacy trust, health, deadline, utilization, and per-request maximum cost.

ModelPrivacyHealthBudget
02

Then score candidates

Eligible providers receive a normalized weighted score. The current sandbox gives cost the largest influence while still accounting for latency, utilization, and model quality.

score = wc·cost + wl·latency + wu·load + wq·quality_penalty
03

Fail over automatically

Provider attempts are deadline-bounded. Errors feed a circuit breaker, and the gateway continues through the ranked candidate list while the request budget remains.
